About Miniscript Studio

Multi-vendor threshold multisig isn’t a silver bullet for self-custody on its own. Resilience comes from trading interactivity (how many parties must cooperate) for fault tolerance: fallback paths that give you a way out under the worst circumstances.

Miniscript makes those trade-offs expressible and analysable. This studio makes them visible: every block is a real spending condition, every wire a real logical relationship, and everything compiles live in your browser.

Blocks

  • Key is a signer (xpub). Reuse one in many paths.
  • Wait is a relative timelock, older(n).
  • Date is an absolute timelock, after(n).
  • All of / Any of / Threshold combine conditions.
  • Hashlock requires a revealed secret, sha256(h).

Under the hood

  • rust-miniscript compiled to WebAssembly: policy compiler, descriptors, addresses, spend planning.
  • LifeHash (Blockchain Commons) visual fingerprints for keys and descriptors.
  • Runs fully client-side; nothing leaves your browser.

Key ownership

Ownership loads a Key Ownership Manifest (.kom.json): your organisation’s public keys, grouped by signer. Tick the signers you control and every key in the design is marked ours, known (a signer you know but don’t control), known device (right fingerprint, account not listed) or unknown. A conflict means a key claims a known signer’s fingerprint and path but its key material is different: treat it as a possible key substitution. Paths then shows which spending paths you can sign alone.

  • Inventories in any layout are converted to the manifest format first; the format is documented in the Key Ownership Manifest spec.
  • Validation is strict: one malformed entry, unknown field or any private key material rejects the whole file, and the message never quotes the value.
  • A key-id-only manifest (no xpubs) loads faster and reveals no addresses.
  • The manifest stays in this tab’s memory. It is never saved, uploaded or put in share links. Only which signers you ticked is remembered.

This is a design and learning tool. Demo keys come from public seeds. Verify any real wallet with your signing devices before funding it.
